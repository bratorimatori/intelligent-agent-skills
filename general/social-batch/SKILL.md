# Batching short posts

Read `~/.claude/voice-profile.md` first.

## Step 1: One idea, not seven

Ask what the week is about. One claim, one story, one thing they learned. Seven
unrelated posts perform worse than seven angles on one idea, because the account
becomes about something instead of about nothing.

Then ask for the raw material: what happened, what the numbers were, what they
got wrong. Short posts live entirely on specifics. There is nowhere to hide.

## Step 2: Vary the format on purpose

Ten posts on one idea, deliberately different in shape. Do not write ten
declarative one-liners.

- **The specific number**, with the thing it disproves
- **The mistake**, told plainly, with what it cost
- **The counter-take**: the thing everyone says, and why it is wrong here
- **The short how**: three steps someone can do today
- **The observation** that only someone doing the work would notice
- **The comparison**: two approaches, honest about the trade
- **The question you genuinely do not know the answer to**
- **The receipt**: a screenshot, a log line, a real artifact
- **The concession**: where your own position is weakest
- **The long one**: a thread that earns its length

## Step 3: Write for the platform

- **X**: the first line is the whole post as far as the timeline is concerned.
  No preamble. Threads need each post to stand alone, because people arrive in
  the middle.
- **Threads**: slightly longer runway, tolerates conversational openings, punishes
  anything that looks like a LinkedIn post.
- **LinkedIn**: the first two lines are visible before the fold. Line breaks
  carry the rhythm. Still no corporate voice.

## Never

- Hashtag stacks
- "Thoughts?" as a closer
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Who else?", "Unpopular opinion:"
- Emoji bullets, unless the voice profile shows they use them
- Restating one post in ten slightly different ways
- Fabricated statistics or invented anecdotes

## Step 4: Hand back with the reasoning

Give the posts in a numbered list, each labelled with its format, so the user can
see the mix and swap out what does not fit. Flag any post that leans on a claim
they have not given you evidence for.
