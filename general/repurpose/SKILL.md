# Repurposing without diluting

This is the highest-leverage skill in the pack and the easiest to do badly. Done
badly it produces five thin versions of one good thing, and the audience sees
the same piece five times.

Read `~/.claude/voice-profile.md` first.

## Step 1: Find what actually travels

Read the source and pull out the parts that stand alone:

- Every **specific number** and what it disproves
- Every **story or incident**, however short
- Every **claim that could start an argument**
- Every **concrete how**: a sequence someone could follow
- Every **thing the writer got wrong** and what it cost
- Every **quotable line** that survives without its paragraph

Most articles yield four to eight of these. Some yield one. If a piece yields
one, say so and produce one derived post rather than padding to five. That
honesty is the difference between a useful skill and a spam generator.

## Step 2: Match the fragment to the format

Do not push everything into everything. Each fragment has a natural home:

| Fragment | Best format |
|---|---|
| A number that overturns an assumption | Single short post |
| A story with a cost | Thread, or a short video |
| A sequence of steps | Carousel, or a how-to short |
| A contrarian claim | Short post, then a longer reply if it lands |
| A mistake and its fix | Email to the list, where candour is welcome |
| A quotable line | Nothing. Quote graphics are decoration. |

That last row is deliberate. A pull quote as an image is not a piece of content.

## Step 3: Rewrite, do not excerpt

A paragraph lifted from an article and pasted into a post reads like a paragraph
lifted from an article. Each derived piece needs its own opening, because it has
no preceding context, and its own ending, because there is nothing after it.

Change what the format demands: shorter sentences for video, a first line that
works cold for social, a personal frame for email.

## Step 4: Space them

Propose a schedule rather than a pile. Same-day publication of five derivatives
of one article is the pattern audiences recognise and mute. A fortnight is
usually right, with the strongest fragment first.

## Step 5: Link back once, at most

One derived piece should point at the source. The rest should stand alone. A
sequence of posts that all funnel to the same URL reads as promotion, because it
is.

## Hand back

List each derived piece with: which fragment it came from, which format, and
which day. Flag anything that felt thin, and recommend cutting it rather than
letting the user discover it after publishing.
